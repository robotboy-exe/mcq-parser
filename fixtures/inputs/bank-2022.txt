IMPROVISED C. R. K. QUESTIONS FOR 2022
(P.M. Onoriode, Unilag)


1. What does the 'cup' mean in the Gospel of Matthew?
A. A water container
B. A temple vessel
C. A metaphor for suffering
D. Crucifixion
E. Blessing


2. The first to preach in Samaria according to the Acts of the Apostles was
A. Peter
B. Paul
C. Stephen
D. Barnabas 
E. Philip


3. As Joseph revealed himself to his brothers, he told them that God allowed him to be sold into slavery so as to
A. stop the famine
B. preserve life
C. punish them
D. seek revenge


4. The parent of Moses belonged to the tribe of
A. Judah
B. Benjamin
C. Levi
D. Ephraim


5. The parents of Moses did the following to ensure his survival except
A. casting him into the river
B. hiding him for three months
C. putting him in a padded basket
D. his sister watching over him


6. These are your gods, O Israel who brought you up out of the land of Egypt! This statement was a manifestation of Israel's
A. unfaithfulness
B. immorality
C. adultery
D. idolatry


7. God repented that He made Saul King because he
A. utterly destroyed the Amalekites
B. told the Kenites to depart from Amalek
C. did not perform God's commandments
D. set up a monument at Carmel


8. Why was Saul pleased when he was told that his daughter, Michal was in love with David
A. Michal was already getting too old to marry
B. Jonathan, David's friend, was Michal's brother
C. He would get a handsome marriage present from David
D. He thought that Michal might be a snare for David
E. He would send David to destroy the Philistines


9. God changed the name of Abraham's wife from Sarai to Sarah when .......
A. God commanded Abraham to go to the land of Canaan
B. Abraham had come to the land of Canaan
C. Sarah gave birth to Isaac
D. God made a covenant with Abraham
E. Sarah was ninety-nine year old


10. Saul wanted to kill David because
A. David killed Goliath
B. David was just an ordinary shephard
C. he refused to play the lyre for Saul
D. Samuel had secretly anointed David as king over Israel
E. Saul regarded David as a danger to his kingship


11. Prophet .......... assisted Hezekiah with prayer
A. Elisha
B. Hosea
C. Jeremiah
D. Isaiah
E. Elijah


12. Saul, the first king of Israel, was of the tribe of
A. Judah
B. Ephraim
C. Manasses
D. Gad
E. Benjamin


13. Moses offered his own life for the people
A. when they were suffering in Egypt
B. at the crossing of the Red Sea
C. when God came down on the mountain and people were afraid
D. after the making of the golden calf
E. when they were afraid to enter the promised land


14. The response to Peter's question, Lord, how often shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? Meant that forgiveness is
A. unconditional
B. limitless
C. conditional
D. countable


15. The prayer of Jesus at Gethsemane shows that He
A. was in severe agony
B. wanted to escape death
C. needed the support of His disciples
D. had been abandoned by God


16. Which of these did not happen immediately Jesus gave up the ghost? The
A. guards were scared and fled
B. saints were risen
C. curtain divided into two
D. earth shook


17. The miracle which touched on ingratitude is the
A. Healing of Bartimaeus
B. Raising of Jairus’ daughter
C. Cure of the ten lepers
D. Cure of the demonic


18. Paul urges the Romans with regard to giving to do so with
A. Joy
B. Zeal
C. Liberality
D. Love


19. The Israelites entered the land of Canaan under the leadership of
A. Aaron
B. Moses
C. Joshua
D. Nadab
E. Abihu


20. The parable of the lost sheep portrays the shepherd as being
A. Loving
B. Careless
C. Foolish
D. Patient


21. The raising of Lazarus shows that Jesus
A. Loved Mary and Martha
B. Was a good friend of Lazarus
C. Was a person of emotion
D. Is the giver of life


22. When Samuel said to Saul, "To obey is better than sacrifice", he meant that
A. Sacrifices are acceptable only if they come from a man who obeys God
B. God prefers sacrifices to obedience
C. God prefers offerings to sacrifice
D. God has barred all sacrifices
E. Fats of rams are no longer desired for sacrifice


23. The phrase, ‘the soul that sins shall die' indicates
A. Personal responsibility
B. Collective responsibility
C. Israel's guilt
D. The increasing sins of Israel
E. God's forgiveness of sins


24. God created man and asked him to dominate all the following except
A. fish of the sea
B. spirits of the earth
C. birds of the air
D. creeping animals


25. Which of the following experience did Joseph not go through? He was
A. sold into slavery
B. sentenced and imprisoned
C. cast into a pit
D. molested and raped


26. Paul's first missionary journey started from
A. Jerusalem
B. Cyrene
C. Samaria
D. Antioch

A. Rehoboam
B. Samuel
C. John
D. Josiah

A. Ehud
B. Sisera
C. Barak
D. Shamgar

A. Joppa
B. Pisidia
C. Antioch
D. Jerusalem

A. Healing of the leper
B. Casting out demons
C. Raising the dead
D. Calming the storm


31. Who was a persecutor of the church before conversion to Christianity?
A. James
B. Barnabas
C. Paul
D. Silas


32. The Lord sent Samuel to Saul to go and destroy the Amalekites because
A. They opposed Israel on their way out of Egypt
B. They were idolaters
C. They disobeyed God
D. Their land had been given to Israel after the battle

A. He was troubled
B. He was excited
C. He sent gifts
D. He became calm

B. the same spirit
C. grace

B. 105 years
C. 85 years
D. 99 years

A. giving and taking is an ideal way of life
B. retaliation should be in equal measure to the offence

C. Amos

A. Peter
